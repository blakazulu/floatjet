# FloatJet

**Your guide to the best tools for remote work**

FloatJet helps remote workers and digital nomads discover the best productivity tools for location-independent work. Whether you're working from a coffee shop in Bali or your home office, we provide honest reviews, in-depth comparisons, and practical guides to help you find the right tools.

---

## What We Cover

- **Project Management** - Tools like Notion, ClickUp, and Monday.com to keep your work organized
- **VPNs for Digital Nomads** - Stay secure and access your content from anywhere in the world
- **Time Tracking & Productivity** - Apps to help you stay focused and manage your time effectively
- **Cloud Storage & Collaboration** - Solutions for storing, sharing, and working together on files
- **Web Hosting & Email Marketing** - Platforms to build your online presence and grow your audience

---

## Our Mission

We believe remote work should be simple. Our goal is to cut through the noise and help you find tools that actually work, so you can focus on what matters most - doing great work from wherever you are.
