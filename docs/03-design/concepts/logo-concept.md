# FloatJet Logo Concept

## Symbolism

The logo combines the two core pillars of the brand:

* **Float:** Represented by soft curves and the "cloud-like" upper portion, symbolizing freedom, flexibility, and remote
  work.
* **Jet:** Represented by the forward momentum, sharp angles, or "wing-like" elements, symbolizing speed, efficiency,
  and productivity.

## Typography

* **Font:** Custom geometric sans-serif (based on Outfit).
* **Weight:** "Float" is lighter/thinner to feel airy; "Jet" is bolder/thicker to feel grounded and fast.

## Colors

* **Primary:** Ocean Deep (#0F4C5C)
* **Accent:** Jet Stream (#38A3A5)
