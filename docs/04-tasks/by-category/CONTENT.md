## 📋 Content Templates

### Money Page Template

**Structure:** (2,500-3,500 words)

1. Introduction (150-200 words)
2. Quick Picks Comparison Table
3. Detailed Reviews (10 tools × 250 words)
4. Full Comparison Table
5. How to Choose Section
6. FAQ Section (5-8 questions)
7. Conclusion
8. Related Articles

**SEO Requirements:**

- Target keyword in H1, first paragraph, URL
- 5-10 internal links
- Schema markup (Review, FAQ)
- Featured image + comparison tables
- Affiliate disclosure at top

### Supporting Content Template

**Structure:** (1,500-2,000 words)

1. Introduction
2. Main Content (3-5 H2 sections)
3. Examples/Case Studies
4. Action Steps
5. Conclusion
6. Related Articles

### SEO Traffic Builder Template

**Structure:** (800-1,200 words)

1. Direct answer to question
2. Expanded explanation
3. Related tips
4. FAQ if applicable
5. Related articles

## ✅ Quality Checklist (For Each Article)

Before publishing any article, verify:

- [ ] Word count meets target (money: 2,500+, supporting: 1,500+)
- [ ] SEO pre-publish checklist complete (40+ items)
- [ ] All affiliate links working with tracking
- [ ] Affiliate disclosure present
- [ ] Images optimized (<200KB, WebP format)
- [ ] Alt text on all images
- [ ] Internal links (5-10 minimum)
- [ ] Schema markup validated
- [ ] Mobile preview looks perfect
- [ ] Lighthouse score >90
- [ ] Spell check passed
- [ ] Fact check completed

## 🎯 Content Strategy

### Month 1 Focus:

- **Priority:** Money pages (10)
- **Goal:** Foundation for affiliate revenue
- **Keywords:** Commercial intent ("best X", "top Y")
- **Target:** Long-tail, low competition

### Month 2 Focus:

- **Priority:** Supporting content (20)
- **Goal:** Topic authority, internal linking
- **Keywords:** Informational ("how to", "guide to")
- **Target:** Medium competition

### Month 3 Focus:

- **Priority:** SEO traffic builders (20)
- **Goal:** Volume traffic, link opportunities
- **Keywords:** Question-based ("can you", "does X")
- **Target:** High volume, low competition

### Ongoing (Month 4+):

- 2-4 new articles per month
- Update top performers quarterly
- Expand based on search data
- Respond to trends
