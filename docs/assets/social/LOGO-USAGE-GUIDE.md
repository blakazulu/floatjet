## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't use `icon_logo_no_bg.png` (with circle) for social profiles**
- Creates circle-within-circle effect
- Use `icon_no_circle_logo_no_bg.png` instead

❌ **Don't resize logos in non-proportional ways**
- Always maintain aspect ratio
- Don't stretch or squash

❌ **Don't add your own circle border to `icon_no_circle_logo_no_bg.png`**
- It's designed to work without one
- Platforms add their own circles

❌ **Don't use low-quality JPG compression**
- Stick with PNG for logos
- Preserve transparency

❌ **Don't modify colors**
- Use files as-is
- Maintain brand color consistency
