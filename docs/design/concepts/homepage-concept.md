# FloatJet Design Concept

Based on the PRD and Master Plan, here is the proposed visual design for FloatJet.

## Design Philosophy

- **Mobile-First:** Prioritizing content legibility and touch targets.
- **Clean & Premium:** Using a teal/blue palette to signify "Float" (freedom) and "Jet" (speed/efficiency).
- **Glassmorphism:** Modern UI elements to feel current and high-tech.
