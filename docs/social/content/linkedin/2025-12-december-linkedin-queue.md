## Post 2: INDUSTRY INSIGHT

**Type:** Observation / Thought leadership
**When:** Wednesday 4/12, 16:00
**Status:** ✅ POSTED
**Format:** Text post
**AI Detection:** 0%

---

Noticed something interesting about remote work tools:

The ones that get recommended the most aren't always the best. They're the ones with the best affiliate programs.

ConvertKit pays $100+ per signup. So every "best email tools" list puts them near the top.
NordVPN's affiliate program is generous. Guess which VPN shows up everywhere.

This isn't necessarily bad—some of these tools are genuinely good. But it means you can't trust rankings at face value.

The question I ask when reading reviews: Did they actually use this? Or did they just look at the commission structure?

You can usually tell. Generic feature lists. No specific experiences. No real opinions. That's the tell.

Worth thinking about next time you're researching tools.

 #RemoteWork #ProductivityTools

Keep getting asked about free VPNs.

My take: skip them.

Free VPN companies still gotta make money somehow. Since you're not paying... they're monetizing you instead. Logging your traffic, selling your browsing history to whoever's buying. Some inject ads into web pages. A few have shipped actual malware.

Calling yourself a privacy tool while selling user data is pretty wild.

Paid VPNs have a simpler arrangement. You give money, they provide service. No sketchy side hustles needed.
