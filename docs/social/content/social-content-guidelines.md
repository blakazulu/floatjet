## Writing Style Guide Reference

Full guide: `/docs/writing_style_guide.md`

### Key Rules for Social Posts

**Voice:**
- Use "I" not "we"
- Conversational, like texting a smart friend
- Slightly edgy, have opinions
- Sentence fragments are fine

**Avoid (triggers AI detection):**
- "Here's the short version:"
- "Here's what I'm looking at:"
- "Question for remote workers:"
- Clean comma-separated lists: "Laptop, phone, tablet, browser."
- Colon-question combos: "Stuff I've been paying attention to: Does it sync?"

**Use Instead:**
- "So I'm building this thing called..."
- "Okay genuine question for anyone..."
- "Probably gonna get pushback on this but:"
- Break lists into natural phrases with personality

**Words to Avoid:**
- "excellent", "exceptional", "comprehensive", "optimal", "robust", "seamless"

**Words to Use:**
- "solid", "fine", "decent", "surprisingly good"
- Or just describe what it does without adjectives

## Twitter Guidelines

### How to Pin a Tweet

1. Post the tweet
2. Click ••• (three dots) on the tweet
3. Select "Pin to your profile"
4. Update pinned tweet monthly

### Best Posting Times (Israel)

- Morning: 15:00-16:00
- Lunch: 19:00-20:00
- Evening: 01:00-03:00

### Hashtag Rules

- Max 1-2 per tweet
- Don't use on every tweet (looks spammy)
- Primary: #RemoteWork, #DigitalNomad, #Productivity, #WorkFromHome

### After Posting

- Engage with replies for 30 minutes
- Reply to every comment

### Next Steps After Each Week

- Check analytics: which tweets got engagement?
- Reply to all comments
- Start following accounts in your niche (after you have content)
- Write Week 2 queue based on what worked

## LinkedIn Guidelines

### How to Add a Featured Post

1. Go to your company page
2. Click "Featured" section (or add it via Edit)
3. Click "+" → Add a post
4. Select the post you want featured
5. This stays visible at top of your page

### Best Posting Times (Israel)

- Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday
- 15:00-17:00
- Avoid weekends

### Hashtag Rules

- Max 1-3 per post
- Mix sizes: 1 large (#RemoteWork), 1-2 medium (#ProductivityTools)
- Don't use same hashtags every post

### Link Strategy

- LinkedIn suppresses posts with links
- Better approach: Post text first, add link in first comment
- Say "Link in comments" at end of post

### After Posting

- Engage with comments for 1-2 hours
- Reply to every comment with follow-up questions

### Next Steps After Each Week

- Check LinkedIn Analytics (Company Page → Analytics)
- Which posts got engagement? Comments vs likes?
- Reply to all comments with follow-up questions
- Adjust tone based on what resonates
- Write next week's queue

## Platform Comparison

| Aspect | Twitter | LinkedIn |
|--------|---------|----------|
| Length | 280 chars | No practical limit |
| Tone | Casual, punchy | Professional but human |
| Frequency | 2-3x/day | 3-5x/week |
| Hashtags | 1-2 | 1-3 |
| Links | In tweet | In comments (better reach) |
| Best days | Any | Tue-Thu |
