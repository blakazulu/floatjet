## Platform Quick Reference

**How to Pin a Tweet:**
1. Post the tweet
2. Click ••• (three dots) on the tweet
3. Select "Pin to your profile"
4. Update pinned tweet monthly

**Best Posting Times (EST):**
- Morning: 8-9 AM
- Lunch: 12-1 PM
- Evening: 6-8 PM

**Hashtag Rules:**
- Max 1-2 per tweet
- Don't use on every tweet (looks spammy)
- Primary: #RemoteWork, #DigitalNomad, #Productivity, #WorkFromHome

**After Posting:**
- Engage with replies for 30 minutes
- Reply to every comment
