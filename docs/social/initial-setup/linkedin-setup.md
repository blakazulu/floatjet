## Content Strategy for LinkedIn

### Posting Frequency

**Target:** 3-5 posts per week

**Recommended Schedule:**

- **Monday:** Industry insight or article share
- **Wednesday:** Tool review or comparison
- **Friday:** Weekly recap, poll, or engaging question
- **Optional Tuesday/Thursday:** Lighter posts, repost top Twitter content

**Best Posting Times (US audience):**

- **Tuesday-Thursday:** 8-10 AM EST (work hours start)
- **Wednesday 12 PM EST:** Lunch break scrolling (peak)
- **Avoid:** Weekends (low engagement for B2B)

### Content Pillars for LinkedIn

**1. Article Announcements (40%)**

- Share new FloatJet articles with professional framing
- Add 2-3 sentence summary
- Use relevant hashtags (1-3 max)

**Example:**

```
Just published an in-depth comparison of project management tools for remote teams.

Tested Notion, ClickUp, Asana, and Monday.com over 60 days. Here's what actually matters when choosing:

[Link to article]

#RemoteWork #Productivity #ProjectManagement
```

**2. Industry Insights (30%)**

- Remote work trends
- Tool announcements/updates
- Productivity research
- Professional commentary on news

**Example:**

```
Remote work stats that caught my attention:

73% of remote workers report better work-life balance (FlexJobs, 2025)

But 52% struggle with collaboration tools.

This gap is why tool selection matters. The right stack doesn't just enable remote work—it makes it actually enjoyable.

What tools have improved your remote work experience?
```

**3. Behind-the-Scenes (20%)**

- Testing process
- How you evaluate tools
- Real experiences, challenges
- Building FloatJet journey

**Example:**

```
Week 3 of testing VPNs for the next review:

Connected from 15 different coffee shops across 3 countries. Measured speeds at different times. Tried to break the encryption (legally, of course).

The amount of variability is wild. Same VPN, different server, 400% speed difference.

This is why I don't trust "fastest VPN" claims without context.

Full results coming next week.
```

**4. Engagement Posts (10%)**

- Polls
- Questions to audience
- Discussion starters
- Asking for input

**Example:**

```
Quick poll for remote teams:

What's your biggest productivity tool pain point?

🔹 Too many tools, scattered workflow
🔹 Team not adopting tools consistently
🔹 Tools don't integrate well
🔹 Paying for features we don't use

(Vote in comments or reply with "Other" + your issue)
```

### LinkedIn Post Formats

**Text Post (Best Engagement)**

- **Length:** 1-3 paragraphs for timeline, longer for deep dives
- **Format:** Short opening line, line breaks for readability, question/CTA at end
- **Hashtags:** 1-3 max (LinkedIn penalizes hashtag spam)

**Link Post (Drive Traffic)**

- **Format:** 2-3 sentence context + link
- **Note:** LinkedIn suppresses reach of link posts (prefers native content)
- **Tip:** Post as text first, add link in first comment (higher reach)

**Image Post**

- Use for infographics, charts, tool screenshots
- LinkedIn favors 1200x627px images
- Add descriptive alt text (accessibility + SEO)

**Document Post (High Engagement)**

- Upload PDFs or multi-page content
- LinkedIn shows carousel preview
- Great for guides, checklists, comparisons
- Can repurpose blog content this way

**Video Post**

- Native video uploads get high reach
- Keep under 3 minutes (LinkedIn sweet spot)
- Add captions (most watch without sound)
- Tool demos, quick reviews, tips work well

**Poll Post**

- Asks question with 2-4 options
- High engagement rate
- Run weekly for audience insights

### Hashtag Strategy for LinkedIn

**Use 1-3 hashtags per post** (max 5, but diminishing returns)

**Mix sizes:**

- 1 large hashtag (500k+ followers): #RemoteWork
- 1-2 medium hashtags (50k-500k): #ProductivityTools
- 0-1 small niche hashtag (5k-50k): #RemoteWorkTips

**Primary Hashtags for FloatJet:**

- #RemoteWork (1.5M+ followers)
- #Productivity (800k+ followers)
- #DigitalNomad (300k+ followers)
- #WorkFromHome (700k+ followers)
- #ProductivityTools (50k+ followers)

**Don't:**

- Use irrelevant broad hashtags (#Business, #Success, #Motivation)
- Stuff hashtags (looks spammy)
- Use same hashtags every post (vary based on content)

## LinkedIn Analytics

**Access:** Company Page → Analytics tab

**Key Metrics to Track:**

### 1. Visitors

- **Page views:** How many people viewed your LinkedIn page
- **Unique visitors:** Individual people (removes duplicates)
- **Visitor demographics:** Job titles, industries, locations

**Goal:** 50+ visitors/week by Month 2

### 2. Followers

- **Follower growth:** Net new followers
- **Follower demographics:** Industries, seniorities, locations
- **How they found you:** Organic search, shares, ads

**Goals:**

- Month 1: 50 followers
- Month 3: 250 followers
- Month 6: 1,000 followers

### 3. Updates (Post Performance)

- **Impressions:** How many times post was seen
- **Clicks:** Link clicks, "see more" expansions
- **Reactions:** Likes, comments, shares
- **Engagement rate:** (Reactions + Comments + Shares) / Impressions

**Engagement Rate Benchmarks:**

- 2-3%: Average
- 4-6%: Good
- 7%+: Excellent

### 4. Competitor Tracking

- Track similar pages (Wirecutter, The Verge, productivity blogs)
- See their follower growth, posting frequency
- Learn from high-performing content

**How to Track Competitors:**

1. Analytics → Competitors tab
2. Add competitor pages
3. Monitor their metrics

## Engagement Best Practices

### Respond to Comments

**Within 1-2 hours of posting:**

- LinkedIn algorithm favors quick engagement
- Reply to every comment (builds community)
- Ask follow-up questions (drives more comments)

**Example:**

```
User: "Great comparison! I've been using Notion for 6 months."

You: "Nice! What's your favorite Notion feature? I found the database views really powerful but took time to learn."
```

### Engage with Others' Content

**Daily engagement routine (15-20 minutes):**

1. Check LinkedIn feed
2. Like 10-15 relevant posts
3. Leave thoughtful comments on 3-5 posts
4. Share/reshare 1-2 particularly valuable posts

**Commenting strategy:**

- Don't just say "Great post!" (low value)
- Add your perspective or experience
- Ask a question or add insight
- Tag relevant people if appropriate

**Example of good comment:**

```
This resonates. I switched from [Tool A] to [Tool B] last month for exactly this reason.

The learning curve was steeper, but the [specific feature] alone saved our team 5+ hours/week.

Have you tested [Tool C]? Curious how it compares.
```

### Join Relevant Groups

**Recommended Groups:**

- Remote Work & Digital Nomads
- Productivity & Time Management
- Freelancers & Solopreneurs
- Digital Marketing
- Product Reviews & Recommendations

**Group Strategy:**

- Join 5-10 groups
- Participate authentically (answer questions, share insights)
- Don't spam your content (contributes value first)
- Share articles when genuinely relevant to discussion

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't

❌ **Only post promotional content** - Add value, share insights, engage authentically

❌ **Ignore comments** - Reply to every comment within 2 hours for best reach

❌ **Post inconsistently** - Algorithm favors regular posters (3-5x/week)

❌ **Use too many hashtags** - Max 3-5, LinkedIn isn't Instagram

❌ **Post and ghost** - Engage with your own post for 1-2 hours after publishing

❌ **Connect with everyone as "floatjet"** - Use personal account to connect, not company page

❌ **Repost Twitter content verbatim** - LinkedIn audience wants more professional, in-depth content

### Do

✅ **Native content performs best** - Don't just link to blog posts, add context

✅ **Engage before posting** - Spend 15 mins engaging with others before posting yours

✅ **Use line breaks** - Wall of text is hard to read; break into short paragraphs

✅ **Ask questions** - Drives comments, which boost algorithm

✅ **Post at optimal times** - Tuesday-Thursday, 8-10 AM EST

✅ **Tag companies occasionally** - When genuinely relevant (might reshare)

✅ **Track what works** - Double down on high-performing content types
