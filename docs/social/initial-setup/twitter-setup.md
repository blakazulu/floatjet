## Content Strategy

### Posting Frequency

**Target:** 2-3 tweets/day

**Optimal Times (US timezone):**

- **Morning:** 8-9 AM EST (people checking before work)
- **Lunch:** 12-1 PM EST (mid-day scroll)
- **Evening:** 6-8 PM EST (after work engagement)

**Tools for Scheduling:**

- **Free:** TweetDeck (native Twitter tool)
- **Paid:** Buffer ($5/month), Hootsuite, Later

### Content Mix (2-3 tweets/day)

**Daily breakdown:**

**Tweet 1: Value/Tip (40%)**

- Remote work tips
- Productivity hacks
- Tool recommendations
- Quick how-to's

**Tweet 2: Engagement (30%)**

- Questions to followers
- Polls about tools/preferences
- Retweet with commentary
- Industry news reactions

**Tweet 3: Promotion (30%)**

- New article announcements
- Tool review highlights
- Website content links
- Comparison threads

### Tweet Formats That Perform Well

**1. Thread (High Engagement)**

```
I tested 7 VPNs for remote work.

Here's what actually worked:

(Thread 🧵)
```

**2. Quick Tip**

```
Remote work tip:

Use Notion for project docs
Slack for quick questions
Zoom for face-to-face

Match the tool to the communication type.

Saves hours of async confusion.
```

**3. Honest Review**

```
Real talk about ClickUp:

It's powerful. Maybe too powerful.

Took me 3 days to set up properly. But now? Everything's in one place.

Worth the learning curve if you hate juggling 5 tools.

Not worth it if you just need a to-do list.
```

**4. Poll**

```
Quick poll for remote workers:

What kills your productivity most?

🔹 Slack notifications
🔹 Zoom fatigue
🔹 No dedicated workspace
🔹 Time zone chaos

(Vote below, curious what everyone's struggling with)
```

**5. Article Promotion**

```
Just tested 5 standing desks under $500.

Surprising winner: [Brand name]

Full comparison + honest pros/cons:
[link]
```

### Hashtag Strategy

**Use 1-2 hashtags max** - Twitter penalizes hashtag spam

**Primary hashtags:**

- #RemoteWork (most relevant)
- #DigitalNomad (your audience)
- #Productivity (broad but searchable)
- #WorkFromHome (high volume)

**Secondary hashtags:**

- #FreelanceLife
- #RemoteWorking
- #RemoteJobs
- #TechTools
- #ProductivityTools

**Don't use:**

- Generic hashtags (#Tech, #Business, #Tips)
- More than 2-3 hashtags per tweet
- Hashtags in every tweet (looks spammy)

### Engagement Strategy

**Reply to:**

- Everyone who replies to your tweets (builds community)
- Relevant conversations in your niche
- Questions about remote work/tools
- Influencers in remote work space (thoughtful, not spammy)

**Retweet:**

- Industry news (add your take with quote tweet)
- User success stories with your recommendations
- Remote work stats/studies
- Tool announcements you've covered

**Quote Tweet:**
Better than straight retweet - adds your perspective

Example:

```
This is spot on.

I switched from [Old Tool] to [New Tool] last month.

The difference in [specific feature] alone saved me 5 hours/week.

[Original tweet]
```

## Analytics & Tracking

### Twitter Analytics (Free, Built-in)

**Access:** https://analytics.twitter.com

**Key Metrics to Track:**

**Monthly:**

- **Tweet impressions:** How many people saw your tweets
- **Profile visits:** People clicking to see your profile
- **Mentions:** Brand awareness
- **Followers gained/lost:** Growth rate

**Per Tweet:**

- **Impressions:** Reach
- **Engagements:** Likes, RTs, replies, clicks
- **Engagement rate:** Engagements / Impressions (aim for 2-5%)
- **Link clicks:** Traffic to floatjet.com

**Best Performing:**

- Which tweets got most engagement?
- What topics resonate?
- What times perform best?
- What formats work (threads, polls, tips)?

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

### Don't:

❌ **Tweet only promotional content** - Follow 70/20/10 rule (70% value, 20% engagement, 10% promotion)

❌ **Ignore replies** - Respond to every mention/reply (builds community)

❌ **Use too many hashtags** - Max 1-2 per tweet

❌ **Post inconsistently** - Daily posting > sporadic bursts

❌ **Buy followers** - Fake engagement tanks your reach

❌ **Copy competitor content** - Add your perspective, don't plagiarize

❌ **Only talk about your site** - Share others' content, engage genuinely

❌ **Post and ghost** - Engage for 30 mins after posting

### Do:

✅ **Reply within 1 hour** - Shows you're active, boosts engagement

✅ **Ask questions** - Drives replies, which boost algorithm

✅ **Use conversational tone** - Match FloatJet's voice (see writing guide)

✅ **Share real experiences** - "I tested X for 3 weeks..."

✅ **Tag brands occasionally** - When genuinely relevant (they might RT)

✅ **Vary tweet types** - Tips, threads, polls, questions, opinions

✅ **Track what works** - Double down on high-performing formats

## Troubleshooting

### Low Engagement?

**Possible causes:**

- Posting at wrong times (test different schedules)
- Not using questions/polls (drives replies)
- Too promotional (add more value tweets)
- Not replying to others (engage first)

**Fixes:**

- Ask more questions
- Post threads (higher engagement)
- Reply to 10 tweets before posting yours
- Use polls weekly

### Not Growing?

**Possible causes:**

- Inconsistent posting
- Not following relevant accounts
- Profile not optimized (bio, header missing)
- Content doesn't provide value

**Fixes:**

- Post 2-3x daily minimum
- Follow 10-20 new accounts/day
- Complete profile optimization
- Focus on educational/helpful content

### Getting Spam Replies?

**Solutions:**

- Turn on quality filter (Settings → Notifications)
- Mute words (common spam terms)
- Block obviously spam accounts
- Don't engage with bots
