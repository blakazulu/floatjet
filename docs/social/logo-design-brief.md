## Design Direction

### Selected Concept: Letter "F" + Jet Trail

**Visual Description:**
A bold, geometric letter "F" with a stylized jet stream or contrail flowing dynamically from the top of the letter, suggesting movement, speed, and freedom.

**Key Elements:**

1. **Letter "F"**
   - Bold, geometric letterform
   - Primary color: Ocean Deep (#0F4C5C)
   - Clean, modern typography (similar to Outfit font)
   - Centered in composition for circle-crop safety
   - Should be instantly recognizable as "F"

2. **Jet Stream/Trail**
   - Flowing from top of "F" (or integrated into the letter)
   - Suggests movement, speed, jet stream concept
   - Uses Jet Stream color (#38A3A5) or gradient (Ocean Deep → Jet Stream → Sky Light)
   - Smooth, flowing lines (not harsh/angular)
   - Dynamic but not chaotic

3. **Overall Composition**
   - Circular or square composition (circle-crop safe)
   - Balanced and centered
   - Negative space utilized effectively
   - Works at small sizes (details don't blur)

**Style:**
- Flat design (no 3D effects, shadows, or gradients unless subtle)
- Minimalist and clean
- Modern SaaS/tech aesthetic
- Professional but not boring
