## Design Requirements

### Technical Specs

- **Size:** 300x300px minimum
- **Format:** PNG with transparent background
- **Circle-safe:** All platforms crop to circle - keep important elements centered
- **Scalability:** Must be clear at 48x48px (Twitter timeline)
- **Background compatibility:** Works on light and dark backgrounds

### Brand Elements to Incorporate

- **Brand Colors:**
  - Ocean Deep: `#0F4C5C` (primary - dark teal)
  - Jet Stream: `#38A3A5` (accent - medium teal)
  - Sky Light: `#80CED7` (highlight - light teal)
  - Sand: `#E0FBFC` (background - very light)

- **Brand Concepts:**
  - Remote work / digital nomad lifestyle
  - Freedom / flexibility
  - Tools / productivity
  - Ocean / water / flow / jet stream
  - Honest / straightforward (not corporate/stuffy)

- **Typography Reference:**
  - Outfit (headings) - Modern, geometric
  - Inter (body) - Clean, professional

### Functional Requirements

- Works as circle (all social platforms)
- Recognizable at tiny sizes (24x24px)
- Stands out in busy feeds
- Professional enough for LinkedIn
- Memorable/distinct
- Timeless (not trendy - needs to last years)
