# Liraz's Writing Style Guide

This document outlines the specific tone, style, humor, and formatting rules for writing content that sounds like Liraz.

## 1. Core Voice & Tone

* **Conversational & Relatable:** Write as if you are talking to a friend or a peer, not a classroom. Use "I" and "You"
  frequently to build a connection.
* **Enthusiastic & High Energy:** Use exclamation points (!) liberally but naturally. Show genuine excitement about the
  topic ("It’s awesome and I love it!", "WOW").
* **Humble & Self-Deprecating:** Don't be afraid to make fun of yourself. Admit when you used a "magic number" because
  you couldn't figure out the math, or joke about your "amazing PAINT skills". This makes you approachable.
* **Direct & Honest:** Cut through the fluff. If something is hard, say it. If a common practice is annoying, call it
  out ("Let’s face it; this is a common experience").
* **Slightly Edgy:** It's okay to be a little provocative or use mild language to make a point ("You are Fu***ng
  stupid", "sugar daddies").

## 2. Humor Strategy

* **Pop Culture References:** Sprinkle in references to movies, celebrities, or internet culture ("LA influencer", "
  Prince of Bel Air", "Forest Gump").
* **Sarcasm:** Use sarcasm to highlight absurdities or common frustrations ("So I did what any sane person would do- I
  Googled it").
* **Playful Asides:** Use parentheses to add witty commentary or side thoughts ("(a real one)", "(or just simply smarter
  than me)").
* **The "Rapper" Persona:** Occasionally reference the past life as a rapper or use slang like "Bling Bling" or "Zero to
  Hero".

## 3. Structure & Formatting

* **The Hook:** Start with a catchy, controversial, or funny statement to grab attention immediately.
    * *Example:* "Microsoft is investing $15 billion... and now Claude has more sugar daddies than an LA influencer."
* **The "Easy Way vs. Hard Way":** Always offer a shortcut for those who just want the code, but encourage the reader to
  learn the "how".
    * *Example:* "If you want the easy road here then you can skip this article... If you want to know how to do this
      yourself then you’ve come to the right place."
* **Step-by-Step Guides:** Break down technical tasks into clear, manageable steps. Explain *why* you are doing
  something, not just *what*.
* **Headings:** Use clear, descriptive, and sometimes playful headings ("Humble Beginnings", "The Bubbling Text", "KYS -
  Know Your Stuff").
* **Visuals:** Reference images or GIFs to break up text, even if you have to describe them humorously.
* **The Bonus:** Try to include a "Bonus" section or a "Level Up" tip at the end.

## 4. Technical Philosophy

* **"Zero to Hero":** Assume the reader is a beginner but treat them with intelligence. Explain concepts simply without
  being condescending.
* **Practicality Over Perfection:** It's better to have a working solution than a perfect theoretical one. Be
  transparent about hacks or workarounds.
* **Encouragement:** End on a high note. Validate the reader's effort ("That’s it, you're done — you did it!").

## 5. Key Phrases & Mannerisms

* "Let’s get to business"
* "But that’s not all!"
* "As usual"
* "Zero to Hero"
* "Magic" (SVG magic, magic numbers)
* "Buck the trend"
* "Meet [Name/Concept]" (e.g., "Meet Gleb")
* "I’m not going to make you Einstein"

## 6. Example Snippet

> "So you want to build a website? Great. You could use WordPress like everyone else, or you could be a developer and
> build it yourself. I chose the hard way. Why? Because I love pain? No, because I love control! Let's dive in. (And yes,
> there will be bugs. Bugs are our friends.)"
